# CABO Learning Hub — Messaging and Language Guide

**Canonical identity:** [Platform Identity and Positioning](PLATFORM_IDENTITY.md)

## Positioning

**Name:** CABO Learning Hub  
**Category:** Connected Education and Lifelong Development Platform  
**Tagline:** Every learner. Every milestone. Every possibility.

**One line:** One connected space for learning, learner growth, institutional support and future opportunity.

**30-second pitch:** CABO Learning Hub goes beyond the traditional LMS. It is an evolving platform that connects the learner's journey with educational workflows—learning, assessment, academic evidence, progress, intervention, institutional reporting and potential pathways beyond school. It aims to help educators and authorised decision-makers see more than a mark and respond to what each learner actually needs. Today's platform includes working prototypes and controlled pilots; official integrations and wider rollouts require institution-specific validation and safeguarding.

**90-second pitch:** Learning technologies too often organise content without preserving the wider learner story. A child grows through classrooms, teachers, subjects, sport, personal achievements and sometimes additional support needs. CABO is being designed to connect these pieces, while keeping the learner's privacy, human oversight and institutional authority central. The platform's current repository demonstrates document learning and exams, role-based institution workflows, academic progress, governance and an initial admissions pathway. Our next work is independently validating these workflows with pilot partners, improving accessibility across devices, and formalising compliant operations in each country. The goal is not to replace teachers or national registers; it is to give the right people more coherent evidence and better opportunities to support learners.

| Audience | Lead benefit | Safe proof / demo |

| Educators | More context for assessment and learner support | Document exercises, exam generation, moderated assessment flow |
| Learners | Continuity of learning and recognition of wider growth | Personal journey, private evidence and learning activities |
| Guardians | More constructive collaboration with schools | Guardian invitation status; explain verification requirements |
| DBE / education departments | Potential visibility and interoperability with existing systems | Synthetic school governance and anonymised concept; no real national integration claim |

| Higher education | Future pathways from school into tertiary study | Fictional programme and applicant draft, not institutional integration |
| Talent partners | Adult-controlled portfolio potential | Candidate-owned evidence with restricted/unreleased disclosure |

## What CABO is / is not

**Is:** a connected education, development and institutional workflow platform in active development and pilot validation.

**Is not:** solely an LMS, an accredited examination body, an official DBE/Ministry register, a diagnosis engine, a child recruitment database, or an autonomous school decision-maker.

## Claim hygiene

- Mark screenshots, fake schools, example results and stage charts **SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA**.
- Label slide claims **AVAILABLE IN DEVELOPMENT / PILOT**, **IN VALIDATION** or **ROADMAP / PARTNER-DEPENDENT**.
- For written exams: AI-assisted marking is draft/advisory; an authorised teacher/assessor remains accountable.
- For applications: private drafts and recorded external handoff are not official admission or submission.
- For qualifications: no institution-recognised credential without verified issuer and digitally signed workflows.
- For guardians: invitations do not automatically provide access to a child's grades.
- For national departments: government involvement requires formal mandates and agreements.
- For multi-platform: do not promise iOS App Store, Android Play Store or Windows distribution until builds, security checks and store processes are verified.

## Preferred terms

Use **learner journey**, **evidence**, **support**, **human-reviewed insight**, **institutional oversight**, **growth**, **continuity**, **privacy by design**, **inclusive access**, **pathways**.

Avoid **AI decides**, **predicts children's future**, **weak learner**, **guaranteed success**, **fully compliant**, **100% secure**, **universally compatible**, **replaces education departments**, or unsupported performance statistics.

## Pilot call to action

“CABO Solutions is seeking a small number of schools and education partners to evaluate defined workflows with synthetic or lawfully authorised data under agreed safeguarding, privacy, technical acceptance and measurement criteria. Pilot outcomes will guide what can be responsibly rolled out next.”
