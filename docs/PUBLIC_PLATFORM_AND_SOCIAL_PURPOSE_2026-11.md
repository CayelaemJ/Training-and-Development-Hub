# CABO Learning Hub | Public Platform Guide and Social Purpose

**Edition:** 23 November 2026  
**Category:** Connected Education and Lifelong Development Platform  
**Status:** Independent product initiative in active development and controlled pilot validation  
**Tagline:** **Every learner. Every milestone. Every possibility.**

> **The question CABO is built around:** What does this learner need next, and how can the right authorised people help?

## What is CABO?

CABO Learning Hub is an evolving platform intended to connect the learning and development journey of a person, rather than reducing education to an isolated grade, course or school year. It combines learning materials, practice, assessment evidence, personal milestones, educator follow-up, authorised institution workflows, reporting and carefully safeguarded future pathways. A learning management system is one part of the idea, not the whole product.

It is **not** an official government register, accredited awarding body, licensed clinical/psychological assessment service, automatic university admissions system or replacement for school statutory records.

## Why society may benefit

Too often, learning context is fragmented between notebooks, teaching platforms, school reports, attendance tools, communication and separate administration. It becomes difficult to make sense of progress across time or ensure that a support request is acted upon. Marks matter, but are not the full story of a child's efforts, circumstances, creativity, development or potential.

This challenge sits within real educational pressures, particularly unequal learning support and foundational literacy:

- In **PIRLS 2021**, **81% of South African Grade 4 learners** did not reach the low international reading benchmark, as reported to Parliament in 2023. [South African Parliament](https://www.parliament.gov.za/press-releases/media-statement-basic-education-portfolio-committee-briefed-reading-and-literacy-study).
- **UNICEF Botswana** says about one-third of primary school learners lack basic literacy skills after four to five years of schooling. [UNICEF Botswana Education](https://www.unicef.org/botswana/education).

These are national educational indicators, **not evidence that CABO has changed outcomes**. Software cannot compensate for insufficient teaching capacity, poverty, language barriers, infrastructure, connectivity or public investment. CABO's intended, testable contribution is more coherent learning evidence, earlier human attention and accountable support processes.

## Six connected pillars

| Pillar | What it connects | Responsible status |
|---|---|---|
| **Learn** | Private study materials, practice quizzes, written exam drafts, assessment | Source implementation and AI-gated pilots |
| **Grow** | Learner journey, milestones, development observations, evidence attribution | Source implementation and pilot |
| **Support** | Teacher-led follow-ups, support requests, guardian participation | Support source implementation; guardian authority gated |
| **Operate** | Courses, school notices, attendance, institution roles and workflows | Source implementation with deployment testing required |
| **Understand** | Academic reporting, permitted institutional views, reviewable AI drafts | Source implementation and validation |
| **Progress** | Possible future transfer of verified learning evidence, tertiary and adult pathways | Pilot/roadmap; no automatic official transfer |

## How a learner-centred journey could work

1. An institution establishes its permitted users, roles, programmes and course contexts. **A test membership does not prove real institutional enrolment.**
2. A learner accesses their own materials or practice and receives appropriate feedback. Their record is private to them and authorised institutional staff.
3. Educators review relevant assessment work and may create an agreed support action. Where AI is configured, generated feedback is **draft advisory material**, not a final high-stakes decision.
4. Academic results, self-reported achievements and human/institution-verified evidence remain different kinds of information, each with its own provenance.
5. School leaders review reporting, attendance and operations **only within their authority**. They should not gain access to unrelated institutions' children.
6. Over time, future transitions to higher education or work could be supported through verified, purpose-limited sharing. **This is not an already accredited portable national record.**

## What each audience can explore

- **Learners:** learning activities, own progress, personal milestones and agency over self-reported evidence.
- **Educators:** assigned classes, review of assessment evidence, teaching support and appropriate follow-up.
- **School leaders:** scoped operations, reporting and governance.
- **Guardians:** relationship/invitation flows. A request or unverified relationship **must not** disclose a child's marks or other confidential records.
- **Education organisations, NGOs and funders:** a potential evaluated pilot around one measurable problem, not unproven deployment scale.
- **Higher education or skills partners:** future pathways that respect consent, age rules and verified provenance.

## A fictional example, not a result claim

A synthetic learner record includes Mathematics scores across three illustrative school periods, alongside a separate algebra support request. An educator reviews the learning context and decides on a next step. The purpose of the example is to demonstrate contextual decisions and role boundaries. **It is not a measured learning improvement study**, and the support event must not be credited for earlier fictional marks.

## Responsible AI and child data

CABO's AI-assisted work is for question drafts, written-assessment support and advisory development plans, subject to configured providers, appropriate human review and further evaluation. No AI model may independently diagnose, finalise consequential grades, determine a child's career or expose a confidential learner profile to employers.

The implementation contains role and institution boundaries, but **source code and CI success are not a certification**. A real learner pilot requires appropriate legal basis and guardian authority, country-specific privacy reviews, security and accessibility acceptance, data retention/deletion processes, school agreements and clear incident responsibilities.

## What is available, and what is not

**Evidence in the repository:** implementations or pilots for learning material, practice, assessment, academic records, attendance, notices, learner journeys, educator support, role-based portals and scoped reporting.

**Still gated, unverified or future-facing:** general public/mobile app-store releases, official university submissions, government integrations, cross-school verified academic portability, recognised credentials, universal guardian verification and production-scale assurances. Employer access to private learner-development records is disabled by default.

We welcome schools, teachers, education NGOs, community organisations, funders and carefully vetted collaborators interested in discussing specific learning and operational challenges. The appropriate first step is a **discovery conversation or clearly labelled synthetic-data demonstration**, followed only when warranted by a scoped, lawful, accountable pilot.

**We are seeking evidence and partners, not claiming validated nationwide impact.**
