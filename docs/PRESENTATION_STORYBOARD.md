## Opening script

“CABO Learning Hub began with learning and assessment, but the challenge we now address is bigger. Education happens over years, across classrooms, institutions and support networks. Our goal is to connect that journey responsibly, so schools can act on better evidence and learners are recognised for more than marks. CABO is being built as a connected education and lifelong development platform. We are here to show what has been developed, explain what still requires validation and explore how a credible pilot can prove value.”

## Proposed pilot demonstration path

1. Display **SYNTHETIC DEMO DATA** before showing records.
2. Show authorised role dashboard and institutional context.
3. Walk through a learner's academic view, assessment and human-reviewed report.
4. Demonstrate school-stage observations and an intervention/hub workflow.
5. Show administrative insight and governance scoping.
6. Explain admission/application draft as a **pilot**, not direct partner submission.
7. End with open release gates, pilot test design and a concrete next step.

## Close / invitation

“We are not asking you to accept a national rollout claim. We are inviting a structured evaluation: defined learners and staff roles, lawful pilot data, measurable outcomes, security and accessibility testing, and a joint decision on what is ready to scale.”

## Presenter red lines

- Do not say CABO replaces SA-SAMS, LURITS or a Botswana government system.
- Do not claim official DBE, ministry, Umalusi, IEB or university approval.
- Do not present fictional schools, learners, reports or admissions as real.
- Do not claim native apps are downloadable until verified builds and distribution exist.
- Do not portray AI marks, psychological profiles or pathways as final decisions.
- Do not imply age/guardian consent, child safeguards or country compliance are complete without testing and legal review.
