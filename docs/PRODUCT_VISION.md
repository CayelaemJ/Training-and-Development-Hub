# Product Vision — CABO Potential & Opportunity Engine

## Opportunity

Academic marks alone do not capture a person's evolving capabilities. CABO aims to help people maintain a portable, personally controlled record of skills, interests, creative work, achievements, development focus and career aspirations. Schools and universities can eventually contribute verified evidence under appropriate authorisations. The current implementation supports voluntary user-written evidence only.

## Audiences

- Learners: create a private lifelong portfolio, explore possibilities and authorise purpose-specific disclosure.
- Schools: administer academics and contribute appropriately verified development evidence with guardian involvement where required.
- Universities: recognise qualifications, student achievement and optional portability.
- Employers: publish job and early-career opportunities; request scoped access with individual approval and contractual subscription.
- Guardians and educators: support development without determining a child's future via an opaque score.

## Phase 1, actually implemented

1. A private self-managed profile: headline, about, aspirations.
2. User-created evidence: skills, strengths, growth areas, projects, achievements, interests and reflections.
3. Organization-owned opportunity listings.
4. Explicit, scoped access requests, approvals, denials, revocations and recorded views.
5. Seven-day employer grants, no automatic approval; read access disabled unless pilot flag is enabled.
6. A separate navigation page; existing school, university and exam functionality remains.

## Not yet implemented

- Automated personality, psychometric or career reports
- Teacher-verified achievement claims and credential issuance
- Age/guardian authorisation
- Subscriptions, invoices, payments and employer due diligence
- Application forms or application management
- School-to-university portable student records
- Longitudinal strengths charts or AI skills evidence extraction
- Identity portability and data export/delete workflow
- Production privacy retention and incident-response processes

## Design constraints

Never equate low marks with low capability. Present growth areas as specific skills someone can practice, not permanent weaknesses. Any recommendations must reference evidence and offer multiple possible paths; users can challenge or suppress them. Employment selection must involve accountable humans and defensible, valid measures rather than unvalidated personality labels.
