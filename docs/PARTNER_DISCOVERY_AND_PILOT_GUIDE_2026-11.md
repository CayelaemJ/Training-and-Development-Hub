# CABO Learning Hub | Partner Discovery and Pilot Conversation Guide

**Prepared:** 23 November 2026  
**Purpose:** Public-facing introduction for schools, educators, social-impact organisations and prospective pilot partners.  
**Product status:** Independent initiative, development and controlled pilot validation. **No accreditation, live national integration or outcome guarantee.**

## The 30-second introduction

CABO Learning Hub is a developing connected education and lifelong development platform. It brings learning and assessment evidence, learner milestones, teacher-led support and authorised school workflows into a more coherent journey. We are meeting education partners to learn where current processes are fragmented, show honest fictional examples and explore whether a small, responsibly governed evaluation might be useful.

## Why we are starting with listening

Every school is different. Some want to spend less time moving information between spreadsheets and reports. Others want better visibility into learning support, assessment review, attendance, school communication or learner growth. We do not assume that CABO fits every situation.

A good first conversation focuses on **one current pain point**, the people who own it, existing systems, child safeguarding responsibilities, data and connectivity realities, and the conditions under which a demonstration or evaluation would be worthwhile.

## What a demonstration might include

| Journey | What to show with fictional data | What not to claim |
|---|---|---|
| Learner | Owned materials, practice or milestones | Official grades or a complete lifelong verified record |
| Teacher | Assigned work, feedback and a support follow-up | Automated final grading or diagnosis |
| School leader | Institution-scoped attendance, notices or reports | Government approval or guaranteed productivity savings |
| Guardian | Invitation and restricted-access status | Automatic ability to read a child's private records |
| Pathways | Private draft application or learner-owned evidence | Official university submission or recognised credential |

All demo names, marks, institutions and outcomes must be clearly labelled **SYNTHETIC DEMONSTRATION DATA**.

## Questions to guide a first meeting

1. Which learner-support or school-information task currently loses the most time or context?
2. Who needs to act on that information and how is follow-up tracked?
3. What systems, records and approval processes already exist?
4. Where do language, disability accessibility, devices and connectivity matter most?
5. What must be true before learner, guardian or educator data could lawfully enter a pilot?
6. What single improvement would make a small evaluation worthwhile?
7. Who would need to approve legal, safeguarding, security, technical and institutional decisions?

## Proposed evaluation process

1. **Discovery:** identify a narrow problem, decision owner and success criteria.
2. **Synthetic walkthrough:** review one or two workflows with fictional accounts and controlled access.
3. **Readiness assessment:** document legal basis, permission boundaries, implementation gaps, accessibility, support and infrastructure.
4. **Pilot design:** jointly decide scope, participants, roles, data handling, time period, incident escalation, baseline measures and responsibilities.
5. **Measured review:** report what was actually demonstrated, test results, usability feedback and areas still unresolved.
6. **Go/no-go decision:** do not widen access until acceptance and formal approvals are obtained.

Possible measures include staff time spent preparing a specified report, time from a support request to first human review, record completeness and usability issues. **No baseline or outcome improvement is claimed yet.**

## A short pilot invitation

> We are seeking education partners interested in examining how better-connected learning evidence and educator workflows might help learners receive appropriate support. CABO Learning Hub is in development; we can begin with a synthetic-data demonstration and a discussion of what a measured, safeguarded pilot would need. We do not claim government endorsement, accreditation or verified impact. Would a 20-minute introductory meeting be useful?

## Partnership and advertising safeguards

- Do not imply that CABO is a formally registered, revenue-generating company, accredited education body or current government contractor.
- Do not use real child images, personal records, results or organisational logos without appropriate permission.
- Do not claim that AI makes school, psychological or career decisions.
- Do not send broad unsolicited electronic direct marketing. Respect applicable privacy/communications rules, permission and opt-out requirements.
- Do not promise pricing, features, formal partnerships, data integration or production access without approval and verifiable delivery.
- Treat an enthusiastic first meeting as a **prospective conversation**, not a signed client or completed pilot.
