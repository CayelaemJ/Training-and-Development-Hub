# CABO Learning Hub — School / University Data Processing Agreement (SCHEDULE DRAFT)
10 October 2026 — legal review required before execution

## Contract parties and classification
Institution: **[LEGAL ENTITY]**; CABO: **[LEGAL ENTITY]**. Complete processing-role matrix for each feature: enrolment; attendance; marks and exams; uploaded content; user identity; optional career features. The institution is not automatically the responsible party for every activity.

## Written instructions and purpose
CABO processes institution-controlled personal information only on documented authorised instructions and for defined educational services, except where law requires otherwise. Any separate CABO responsible-party processing requires distinct disclosure.

## Technical and organisational measures
Attach evidence-backed schedules for access control, MFA for privileged users, encryption in transit/at rest, separation of tenants, secure software lifecycle, vulnerability management, logging, backup/restore, incident response, data retention, deletion and personnel confidentiality. Describe only implemented, tested controls.

## Children and authorisation
Institution and CABO allocate responsibility for notices, POPIA section 35 authorisations/exceptions, guardian checks, safeguarding and subject-access handling. No employer disclosure of historical minor records as a default.

## Providers and international transfers
List every actual subprocessor, purpose, jurisdiction, onward transfers and transfer safeguards; agree notice/objection process and restrictions on new vendors. No AI vendor training on institution records except if legally approved and explicitly contracted.

## Assistance and incidents
Promptly notify the institution of suspected compromise, preserve evidence, support POPIA section 22 risk assessment/notifications, cooperate with information-access requests, and document escalation contacts. Define concrete reporting times contractually only after operational ability is confirmed.

## Return and deletion
Specify category-level retention, legal holds, export format, end-of-service return/deletion, backup expiry and verification. Academic records cannot be deleted contrary to lawful preservation requirements.

## Audit and liability
Audit evidence rights, subcontractor obligations, security tests, breach cooperation, insurance and proportionate allocation of liability must be negotiated with counsel and institutional procurement.
